Our Mission

StudentHub empowers students with easy access to attendance records, timetables, fee status, events, and university announcements from a single, accessible web platform.

For Students

Track academic progress, apply for leave, register for events, and submit feedback.

For Faculty

Publish announcements and view student participation in campus activities.

For Administrators

Manage registrations, approve events, and monitor portal usage through the admin panel.
