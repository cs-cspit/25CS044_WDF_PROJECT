Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common StudentHub queries

Home › FAQ

How do I reset my portal password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the email instructions sent to your registered address.

Where can I view my attendance?

After logging in, open the Attendance page from the dashboard sidebar to see subject-wise records.

How do I pay semester fees online?

Go to Fees in your dashboard, select pending dues, and click Pay Now to use the secure payment gateway.

Can I apply for leave through the portal?

Yes. Use Leave Application on the dashboard, fill in dates and reason, and submit for approval.

How do I register for campus events?

Visit the Events page to browse upcoming activities and register for open events.
